If no, this is disqualification risk, not lost points, and we would need to decide by about Oct 4 whether a capped mainnet deployment is worth the security, legal and key-handling risk. The solo pivot helps here: a missed stake goes to a cause or a person the user picks, never to Rituals or to other participants, so the old "escrow pooled among friends" framing no longer describes the main path. Group mode still redistributes forfeits among confirmed friends, so any answer about gambling or financial-services review should be checked against that mode. If they require age gating, extra disclosure or a category, we put it in the store listing and privacy text now, before the 30-day publication clock starts. If AI is required, we are exposed and need to decide whether to build real AI or accept it. If AI and SKR are only scored, we do not change the product for rubric points.
