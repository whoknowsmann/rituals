# Rituals: demo video script (CLOCK IN)

Target length: 106 seconds. Built only from adb screen recordings of the app on a Seeker, plus caption cards. Every claim traces to docs/submission/FACTS.md.

## 1. Premise

Rituals lets you put a stake on a weekly promise. Stamp the week and you get the stake back; miss it and that week's stake goes to a cause you chose.

The video is one real run on a real phone, with the waiting cut out and the two wallet approvals marked honestly with caption cards.

The sentence to leave in a judge's head: Show up and you get your stake back, miss and it goes to a cause you chose, and Rituals takes nothing.

## 4. Do not show, do not say

Do not call the name suggestions smart or generated, and never use the two-letter label for them that FACTS forbids. They are optional, deterministic and on-device. Do not describe them at all unless the screen forces it.

Do not imply real money, and do not name any network except devnet. The words are devnet and test tokens. Show the footer "Devnet · test tokens" once, in row 2, and say "devnet, with test tokens" once, in row 13.

Do not use anything on the FACTS "Never" list under Words. Do not describe a missed stake as anyone's gain. Keep the words: your stake, show up, keep your word, get it back, stamp, ritual, week, cause.

Do not show or mention anything on the FACTS "What is hidden" list. If any of it appears on a screen during recording, re-record the shot.

Do not say or show an activation fee. There is none, and the fee settings are 0 / 0 / 0.

Do not say a week passes in less than 6.5 minutes. Say "demo speed" every time timing comes up, and never imply Real weeks was recorded. If asked: real weeks are 7 days, and a finished week settles the next time the app opens.

Do not say the stamp note is on chain. It stays on the phone.

Do not say the missed-week shot was recorded in this run. Row 12 is an existing ritual, and the table says so. If a caption is wanted there, "From an earlier ritual" is accurate.

Do not show the wallet. It records black, and the requester name it shows is "github.com", which we have not fixed yet. The caption cards stand in for it.

Do not say Seed Vault Wallet is the only wallet. The app offers Phantom too. Just don't mention it in this video.

## 5. Recording checklist

Who does what: Hermes drives the phone over adb and records the screen. Jack approves in the wallet by double-tapping the side button and using his fingerprint. Jack records the voiceover afterwards.

Mode: day mode. The app's warm paper background and the deep-green KEPT ink are built for it, they compress better than a dark screen, and the stamps read clearly at video bitrate. Set the phone to light, or tap the sun/moon toggle in the top bar, and confirm before the first take. Do not switch mid-recording.

Before the first take:
1. Seed Vault Wallet is switched to Devnet. Otherwise the app shows "Network mismatch".
2. The wallet holds at least 5 TEST plus enough SOL for the create, about 0.01 to 0.02 SOL for a 4-week ritual.
3. Home already has one finished ritual that shows a miss: a tile reading "1 of 4 kept", with a MISSED stamp inside. Open it once and check the line naming where the stake went, and that nothing hidden appears on it.
4. Home also has one ritual with a kept week, so row 2 shows two different tiles and a status pill reading "Stamp this week" if possible.
5. Notifications off, battery and clock tidy, no other apps in the recent tray.
6. Scroll Home to the bottom once and confirm the footer "Devnet · test tokens" is visible.

The run:
1. Start recording. Open Home, then "+ New ritual".
2. Type "Read" as the ritual. (FACTS shows "to read" becoming "Read". Try both in a dry run and use whichever lands on "Read" cleanly.)
3. Step 2: 4 weeks, Demo speed on, stake 5. Check the TEST balance line, the locked cause "Northside Food Bank (demo)", and the signed card.
4. Tap Lock it in. First approval, within 60 seconds: Jack double-taps the side button and uses his fingerprint. Approvals must land within about 60 seconds, so Jack should be holding the phone and ready before Hermes taps.
5. Wait for "Locked in." Capture the "Demo speed: a week here is 6.5 minutes." line and the countdown "Week 1 opens in 1:58" on the ritual screen. Keep recording through the wait; cut it in the edit.
6. When the countdown ends and HOLD appears, Hermes presses and holds for the full 1.4 seconds. Add a one-line note. Second approval, within 60 seconds: Jack approves. The stamp window is 5 minutes, so do not start this late.
7. Keep the recording running through confetti, SHOWED UP and the sayings line.
8. Stay on the app until the week closes and the app settles by itself, about a minute after the stamp time runs out. No approval is needed. Capture the KEPT receipt with the note.
9. Stop. Record the missed-week shot separately from the existing ritual.

Rough timing for the run: create, then about 2 minutes to week 1, then up to 5 minutes to stamp, then about 1 minute of grace. Plan on 10 minutes of real recording for one good take.

Stills for the caption cards: grab a clean frame before each wallet approval (step 2 with the Lock it in button, and the stamp screen). Add the card text in the edit; the app never shows it.
