Rituals UX review — 2026-10-06
Basis: attached contact sheet and source at e63f109; screenshot filenames inspected only. No install, build, device access or runtime test. The current stamp is YOU SHOWED UP!; the widget is unshipped and not assessed.

Verdict
Yes: the warm paper, Fraunces, coloured punch cards and ink stamp feel authored and memorable; Create 2 and Finished still fall back into stacks of rounded boxes.
Product-market fit is unproven: voluntary accountability for wallet users, not proof of doing the activity. Innovation is in the enforced consequence, not habit tracking; another wallet approval every week must earn its friction.
Biggest five-minute risk: the clock outruns comprehension. Wallet detours and invisible deadlines can turn a judge's first ritual into all misses before they experience their stake coming back.

First-run judge path
Install: not tested here. README still has release/contact placeholders and omits connecting in its trial steps; the APK handout must put wallet Devnet setup before the first action, not leave discovery to an error.
Connect: Home offers Seed Vault Wallet/Phantom, but Start one works disconnected. Create then says to connect without a connect control; Settings has the same dead end. Connection failure is tucked inside the dismissed Home menu.
Get test tokens: 50 tSKR plus 0.05 SOL, no signature, is the right shortcut. But Create hides its faucet button after ANY response, including failure; balance re-read waits four seconds, and loading buttons display only “…”. Enough tSKR does not establish enough SOL.
Create: one approval locks all weeks, but the 45-second activation lead starts at preparation, BEFORE the wallet opens. “Approve within a minute” is unsafe. The github.com requester and unexplained additional SOL cost undermine trust at the hand-off.
Wait for week 1: Locked in. is a separate success screen requiring See your ritual while time keeps running. “In under a minute” is not a countdown; there is no pause while a judge reads or explores.
Stamp: the optional note comes BEFORE the 1.4-second HOLD, which immediately opens the wallet. The 45-second stamp window includes the hand-off, but no deadline is displayed. The confirmed stamp is the payoff; do not dilute it with more narration.
Week closes: 45 seconds to stamp, 10 seconds grace, then a five-second gap. Ignoring every week produces every week missed; deadlines never pause. The funded app key settles while the ritual screen runs/catches up on return, not through a guaranteed background service. Four weeks alone reach final settlement eligibility at about 4m41s from preparation, before setup and transaction latency.
Receipt: closure exposes a link, not an automatic receipt reveal. Only the latest closed week is reachable; a judge returning after later misses cannot tap back to their kept week. The video currently promises a different sequence and obsolete timings.

Screens — ranked by impact; keep / cut / change
1. Ritual: KEEP the poster, hold and confirmed stamp; CUT the completed-state timing line and “Every week is settled.” box; CHANGE to action-adjacent deadlines, primary receipt access and a quiet removal link, not equal-weight buttons.
2. Create 2: KEEP explicit cause choice and “(demo)” labels; CUT separate oversized containers for every recipient; CHANGE to compact radio rows, TOTAL STAKE and validation after interaction. A custom wallet needs a pre-existing tSKR account: disclose this at selection, not after pasting.
3. Home: KEEP the coloured punch-card grid; CUT the coming-soon line; CHANGE to active/due rituals first and New ritual within reach. “Stamped” beside “0 of 1 kept” reads like failed progress; show the closing time instead of the pending kept count.
4. Receipt: KEEP distinct KEPT/MISSED marks and the personal note; CUT duplicate outcome headings/amounts; CHANGE to include the ritual name and one clear amount/destination line. Make closed week cells tappable; Back to rituals currently returns to the ritual, not Home.
5. Create 1: KEEP the question, input and breathing room; CUT the compulsory-looking expansion of suggestions after typing; CHANGE Suggestions to opt-in. The three-times-a-week placeholder implies tracking the app does not provide; one weekly self-report is the actual contract.
6. Settings: KEEP test tokens and explicit Devnet disclosure; CUT the repeated large Settings title beneath the navigation title; CHANGE to put the faucet first and technical information behind a quieter link. A program Explorer link is not evidence of an individual receipt.
