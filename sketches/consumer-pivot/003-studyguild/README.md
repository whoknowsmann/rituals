## Variant: StudyGuild — crisp collaborative progress

### Design stance
Make peer momentum visible and let AI feel like a shared study instrument, not a chatbot.

### Key choices
- Layout: guild progress dashboard with one dominant daily action.
- Typography: compact, direct system typography.
- Color: paper, ink, periwinkle, yellow, and mint.
- Interaction: open an AI-generated quiz and answer a question.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: daily retention, useful AI demonstration, clear progress.
- Weak at: risks resembling an existing study app unless group unlocks remain central.

### Best for
Small exam, certification, and language-learning crews.
