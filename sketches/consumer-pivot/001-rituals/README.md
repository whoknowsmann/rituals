## Variant: Rituals — warm ritual tracker

### Design stance
Emotional and grounded: a shared ritual should feel like a keepsake, not a financial contract.

### Key choices
- Layout: single-column phone dashboard centered on the next group ritual.
- Typography: soft, high-contrast rounded system typography.
- Color: forest, parchment, clay, and muted gold.
- Interaction: open the ritual creator, choose a ritual family, and advance.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: warmth, repeat-loop clarity, camera/location check-in framing.
- Weak at: the Solana/settlement mechanism is intentionally subordinate.

### Best for
Friend groups, couples, clubs, and families maintaining a real-world recurring practice.
