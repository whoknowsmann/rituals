## Variant: QuestTable — theatrical group reveal

### Design stance
The event should feel like a tiny live show, with contributions building toward a visible shared reveal.

### Key choices
- Layout: single-screen constellation showing group progress.
- Typography: editorial serif paired with compact system labels.
- Color: plum, rose, and gold for an after-dark gathering feel.
- Interaction: open the final quest, submit the table verdict, and complete the artifact.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: memorable demo, group participation, premium-pack identity.
- Weak at: risks making people stare at the phone during an in-person event.

### Best for
Dinner parties, birthdays, clubs, and small private gatherings.
